# Product Context: Balão da Informática Castelo

## 1. Target Audience & Core Value Proposition
- **Audience**: Gamers, professionals, students, IT enthusiasts, and local businesses in Campinas and the Metropolitan Region (Sumaré, Hortolândia, Paulínia, Valinhos, Vinhedo, Indaiatuba).
- **Core Value**: Local physical store trust in Cambuí + e-commerce agility. Retirada no balcão no Cambuí (consulte disponibilidade imediata no WhatsApp), assistência técnica com bancada própria, 10% de desconto no PIX e parcelamento em até 10x sem juros.

## 2. Brand Identity & Visual Rules
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Accent: **Vermelho Balão Oficial** (`#E60012` / `rgb(230, 0, 18)`)
  - Pure Light: **Branco Puro** (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Deep Canvas (Dark Mode): **Carbon Slate** (`#090d16` / `#0f172a`)
  - Panel Surface: **Deep Navy Slate** (`#111827`)
  - Card Surface: **High-Contrast Dark Slate** (`#161f32`) & **Pure White Photo Wells** (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Text: **Pure White** (`#FFFFFF`), **Muted Slate** (`#94a3b8`), **Subtle Slate** (`#64748b`)
- **Strict Negative Constraints**:
  - ❌ NO purple-to-blue AI gradients.
  - ❌ NO green or yellow accent colors.
  - ❌ NO gray text on colored backgrounds.
  - ❌ NO nested card-inside-card clutter.
  - ❌ NO cramped buttons or text overflowing boxes.
  - ❌ NO white product images with contrasting white box borders on dark cards — photos must blend seamlessly into crisp white background wells or transparent surfaces.

## 3. Product Shelves Ordering Standard
1. 🚀 **Computador Gamer & Setups**
2. 💻 **Notebooks & Laptops**
3. 🖥️ **Monitores Gamer & UltraWide (Full Size Showcase)**
4. 📱 **Smartphones & Celulares 5G**
5. ⚡ **Hardware & Peças para Upgrade**
6. 🎧 **Periféricos & Setup Gamer**
7. 🎮 **Consoles, Games & Acessórios**
- **Sorting Rule**: In every shelf, products are sorted strictly from **Most Expensive to Least Expensive** (`price DESC`).
